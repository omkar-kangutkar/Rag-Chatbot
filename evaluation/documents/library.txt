Brookford Library is located at 7 River Road in the fictional town of Brookford.

The library opens Monday to Friday from 10 am to 6 pm.
On Saturdays, it opens from 10 am to 2 pm.
It is closed on Sundays.

Library membership is free for Brookford residents.
Members can borrow up to five books at a time.
Books must be returned within three weeks.
A book can be renewed once if nobody else has reserved it.

Printing costs 10p per page in black and white.
Colour printing costs 40p per page.

A free children's storytelling session takes place every Saturday at 11 am.
Children must be accompanied by an adult.
