Willow Cafe is located at 18 Market Street in the fictional town of Brookford.

The cafe opens Monday to Friday from 8 am to 5 pm.
On Saturdays, it opens from 9 am to 3 pm.
It is closed on Sundays.

A cappuccino costs £3.20, a tea costs £2.00, and a cheese sandwich costs £4.50.
Oat milk is available for an extra 40p.

The cafe offers free Wi-Fi and has six outdoor tables.
Dogs are welcome in the outdoor seating area only.

Customers can reserve a table by calling 01632 960123.
Groups of more than six people must book at least one day in advance.
